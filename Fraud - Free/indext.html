Community Safety & Prevention Tips

Learn how to identify common social engineering tactics and stay secure online.

Never Share Passwords or PINs

Legitimate financial institutions or service providers will never ask for your secret PIN, passwords, or full account credentials over phone or SMS.

Inspect Links Carefully

Always double-check URL spellings. Fraudsters often use look-alike domains (e.g., `amaz0n.com` instead of `amazon.com`) to steal login details.

Beware of False Urgency

Scammers create fake emergencies ("Your account will be blocked in 1 hour!") to pressure you into acting before thinking.
